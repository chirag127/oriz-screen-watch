How the value score works

For every tracked stock we take the cheapness factors it exposes on screener.in — E/P (1/PE), B/P (1/PB), S/P (sales-to-price) and Dividend Yield — convert each into a cross-sectional z-score across the universe, and average them (a stock missing a factor is averaged over the rest). The average z is mapped to a score of 1+z when positive, else 1/(1−z), following the NSE Nifty500 Value 50 construction. Higher = cheaper. The Enhanced ranking drops dividend yield (BSE-Enhanced-Value style). Ratios absent from the page are shown as —, never fabricated.
